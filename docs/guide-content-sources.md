# Guide pack source and rights audit (2026-11-27)

This record covers `bird-th` v8, `butterfly-th` v5, and `butterfly-vn` v4. They remain **preview checklists**, not reviewed identification guides. Existing pack IDs, species IDs, and sightings are preserved. The migration removes generated claims where no species-level evidence was recorded; an empty identification key is intentional. Manifest licences now say "Mixed; see item-level licences and provenance" so they cannot be read as a blanket CC BY-SA claim over all media.

## Reproducible taxonomy correction

`scripts/checklist_ingest.py` combined GBIF Thailand occurrence facets and iNaturalist observation counts. The Thailand pack compiler filled missing accounts with generic habitat, markings, host/call text and all-year seasonality. `scripts/compile_full_vietnam_checklists.py` built the Vietnam list from a cached Indochina Wikipedia list plus iNaturalist/GBIF observations; its manifest had incorrectly suggested direct use of published Vietnam butterfly books. `scripts/compile_full_master_packs.py` could default a missing Vietnam family to Nymphalidae and generated family/name-based field traits. These paths are evidence of *candidate occurrence and taxonomy*, not species-level field accounts.

The committed [`GBIF match snapshot`](../data/taxonomy/gbif-family-2026-11-27.json) contains the response and URL for 2,317 names. To refresh it deliberately, run `python3 scripts/fetch_gbif_family_snapshot.py`; the normal build does not contact GBIF. Then run `python3 scripts/apply_preview_content.py --write`, inspect the diff, and run `python3 scripts/check_pack_release.py`. A correction requires a strict `EXACT` species match, identical canonical binomial and class, confidence at least 95, accepted or synonym status, and a family ending `idae`. Fuzzy, higher-rank, uncertain and failed matches are not applied. The matching rule and [GBIF Species API](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/openapi/v1/species) are machine-verifiable; GBIF itself cautions that [taxonomy matching is interpretation](https://techdocs.gbif.org/en/data-processing/taxonomy-interpretation), so this is not expert species validation. The [GBIF Backbone](https://www.gbif.org/dataset/d7dddbf4-2cf0-4f39-9b2a-bb099caae36c) is CC BY 4.0; this snapshot only reuses scientific-name and family facts with recorded usage keys.

The butterfly key is limited to wingspan; no colour/pattern key is claimed. ## Plate rights and remaining work

Thailand plate metadata records each reference photo's licence and URL when available. The previous plate pipeline sometimes assigned a CC BY-SA label to a drawing despite an All Rights Reserved or unrecorded reference licence. A credit or generated drawing does not itself establish a derivative grant. [iNaturalist's photo rights guidance](https://help.inaturalist.org/en/support/solutions/articles/151000169918) says media rights belong to uploaders and are determined per photo. This migration retains that audit metadata, but shows an archetype instead of a photo-based species plate when the reference is All Rights Reserved, NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, unrecorded, or an unknown licence code. The release gate enforces this allowlist. The surviving displayed Thailand plates have recorded CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public-domain references, with per-image credit and licence metadata; those attributions should still be checked against the original source before calling the packs complete.

The 23 Thailand images marked as placeholders while still showing traced drawings have recorded CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC0 references; the flag also records their editorial quality status. Category counts above include previously existing placeholders, so they are not additive to the placeholder column. Thailand has **1,119 NonCommercial reference species** (487 birds, 632 butterflies) requiring either per-photo permission for the intended use or replacement by independently sourced imagery. The original reference URLs and licence codes remain in each row's `provenance.plate` for review. Thailand also has 399 All Rights Reserved, 150 NoDerivatives, and 663 unrecorded reference species in placeholder metadata; these may overlap older archetype fallbacks. In Vietnam, 567 generated plates plus 365 traced drawings had no recorded reference rights and are no longer referenced by the new pack version. A rights reviewer must establish their source lineage or replace them before a complete release. Previously published or R2-hosted media are outside this repository change and require a separate inventory/removal decision; old installed packs may still contain earlier plates.

To finish these guides, a qualified editor must still provide licensed, species-level visual identification features and row-by-row regional review; fill the remaining habitat, seasonality, hostplant/call and taxonomy gaps documented in the source/rights matrix; resolve the 177 Thailand unknown families and 284 Vietnam taxonomy exceptions; and review or replace each restricted or untraceable plate. The current gate prevents the known generated claims and restricted Thailand plates from returning silently, but cannot certify biological accuracy or legal reuse of a source supplied later.
